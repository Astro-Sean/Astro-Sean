Hi, I'm **Seán**, an astronomer at the [Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics (MPE)](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/personnel/144270) in Garching, Germany. I study the final years of massive stars: the outbursts they produce before collapse, the circumstellar material (CSM) those eruptions build, and the interacting transients that result. My work focuses on catching massive stars in outburst and reading what their CSM says about the driving physics.

**Research focus**

* Real-time discovery: filters on the [Lasair](https://lasair.lsst.ac.uk/) broker that select pre-SN outbursts and late-time rebrightenings of stripped-envelope SNe, built for the [Rubin/LSST](https://www.lsst.org/) era.
* Spectroscopy of interacting and eruptive transients: PI time on VLT/X-shooter, NOT/ALFOSC and GTC, plus ePESSTO+ classifications.
* Non-LTE spectral modelling of interacting SNe, linking hydrodynamic interaction models ([STELLA](https://github.com/Astro-Sean/OpenStella-PARDISO)) to atmosphere spectra ([CMFGEN](https://github.com/Astro-Sean/cmfgen-nonmono)).
* Multi-wavelength photometry with [MPI 2.2m/GROND](https://www.eso.org/sci/facilities/lasilla/telescopes/national/2p2.html) and X-ray data from [eROSITA](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/eROSITA) and the [Einstein Probe](https://www.einsteinprobe.org/).
