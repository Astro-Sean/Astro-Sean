
Hi, I'm **Seán**, an astronomer at the [Max Planck Institute for Extraterrestrial Physics (MPE)](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/personnel/144270) in Garching bei München, Germany. I study mass loss in massive stars and the nature of interacting transients.

**Research Focus**
I focus on the final stages of massive stellar evolution, particularly pre-supernova activity, to understand what happens in the weeks and months leading up to core collapse. My work generally covers:

* High-energy astrophysical transients, with a focus on interacting systems and their spectral signatures
* Multi-wavelength photometric analysis using instruments like the [MPI 2.2m/GROND](https://www.eso.org/sci/facilities/lasilla/telescopes/national/2p2.html)
* X-ray studies using data from [eROSITA](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/eROSITA) and the [Einstein Probe](https://www.einsteinprobe.org/)
* Building tools to detect early-time progenitor variability, helping us prepare for next-generation surveys like [LSST](https://www.lsst.org/)

**Open Science & Software**
I'm a strong believer in open-source software because it makes science more transparent and reproducible. I build and share tools to help streamline transient discovery and photometric analysis workflows.
